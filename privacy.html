Privacy Policy

Your Information,
Protected With Care

At MUSWIT, we value your trust and are committed to protecting the privacy of every visitor, partner, and supporter.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us, donate, subscribe, or partner with MUSWIT. This may include your name, email address, phone number, organization details, and communication preferences.

2. How We Use It

We use this information to respond to inquiries, process donations, provide updates, coordinate partnerships, and improve the experience of our visitors and supporters. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Website Analytics

Like many websites, MUSWIT may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use our website, improve functionality, and measure campaign effectiveness. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Data Protection

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to help protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We may share limited information with trusted service providers who assist with website hosting, communications, or donation processing, strictly for the purpose of delivering our services and operations.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. If you have questions about how your data is handled, please contact us using the details below.

Contact

Questions About Privacy?

If you have any concerns or questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact MUSWIT at info@muswitgh.com or visit our Contact page.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with the latest revision date.
